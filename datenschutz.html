3. PostHog Analytics

This website uses PostHog, a web analytics service hosted via the EU region (eu.posthog.com). PostHog is only activated after your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); without consent, no usage data is collected or transmitted.

Once consent has been given, PostHog collects anonymized usage events (e.g. page views, clicks, scroll depth, and time on page) as well as session recordings (session replays) of your interaction on this website. Input fields are masked by default.

Collected event data is stored at PostHog for 1 year (Free Plan). Session recordings are stored for 30 days (Free Plan).

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time with one click via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of the respective page. Alternatively, you can contact us by email.

For more information, see PostHog's Privacy Policy.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing of your personal data, as well as the right to data portability. To exercise these rights, please contact the email address given above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In North Rhine-Westphalia, this is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information NRW.
